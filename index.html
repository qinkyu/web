Where human vision and AI synergy unite

Nine G is a creative startup crafting games and media art with AI at the heart of its creative process.

We combine human imagination with AI-driven experimentation to explore new worlds, develop distinctive visuals, and create meaningful interactive experiences. From the first spark of an idea to the final experience, AI helps us expand creative possibilities and bring ambitious concepts to life.

Through games that invite discovery and media art that inspires curiosity, we create new ways for people to play, feel, and connect.
